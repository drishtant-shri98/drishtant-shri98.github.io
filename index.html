One Grid. Very Different Operating Profiles.

A reproducible analysis of Canadian electricity capacity against actual generation, built from Canada Energy Regulator open data. Installed capacity turns out to be a poor proxy for what a fuel type really contributes, and the gap between the two is the story. Written as a self-contained document, so every figure rebuilds from the raw source.

R · R Markdown · Canada Energy Regulator open data
